Do I need an account?

No. Vesper has no accounts or sign-in. Your birth details and your Journal stay on your device.

How is my Card of the Day chosen?

It's drawn once per day from a seed unique to your device, so it stays the same all day (and differs from anyone else's). Open the Today tab and turn the card over to reveal it.

How do I get a personal horoscope?

On the Stars tab, tap Add your details and enter your birth date, time and place. Adding your birth time unlocks your Rising sign and a more precise Moon.

How do I remove my birth details?

On the Stars tab, tap the ··· menu on your reading and choose Remove. Everything is stored only on your device, so it's gone immediately.

Can I turn reversed cards off, or get a new daily card?

Yes. Settings has a Use reversed cards toggle, and Reset today's card draws a fresh Card of the Day for the rest of the day.

Are the readings "real"?

Vesper is made for reflection and enjoyment — a calm daily habit and a beautiful deck, not a prediction of the future. Take what's useful and leave the rest.
