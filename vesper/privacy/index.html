Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 July 2026

Vesper is a tarot and horoscope app. This document describes what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Short version

  • Vesper does not build a profile of you. No accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, and no tracking across other apps.
  • The tarot side is entirely on your device and works offline. Cards, spreads and readings are generated on the phone.
  • For horoscopes, the birth date, time and place you enter are sent to Vesper's astrology service to calculate your chart and reading. They aren't tied to any account and are never used for advertising.
  • Vesper shows a single banner ad via Google AdMob in non-personalised mode — your IDFA is never used and the App Tracking Transparency prompt is never shown.

What stays on your device

The following are stored locally on your device only, using the operating system's standard app storage. They never leave your device:

  • Your birth details (date, time and place), if you add them, so you don't have to re-enter them
  • Your saved readings and reflections (your Journal)
  • Your Card of the Day, its per-device seed, and the reversed-cards setting

If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

Your horoscope (what is sent over the network)

When you ask for a personal reading, Vesper sends the birth date, time and place you entered (the place as map coordinates) to its astrology service:

https://zesty-backend.hjatte.workers.dev

The service uses those details to calculate your natal chart (Sun, Moon, Rising and today's transits) and compose a reading, then returns the result to your device. The request carries no name, no account and no device identifier — there are no accounts in Vesper. This information is used only to generate your reading; it is not linked to you and is never used for advertising.

Vesper also fetches the day's general horoscopes for all twelve signs. That request sends nothing about you.

When you type a birth city, Apple's standard geocoding service converts it to coordinates. Vesper does not access your device's current location.

On-device intelligence

On supported devices, Vesper can optionally use Apple's on-device Foundation Models to phrase a reading or answer a follow-up question. This runs entirely on your device — nothing is sent to Apple, to us, or to any server for it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Vesper displays a single banner ad pinned to the bottom of most screens. The ad is served by Google AdMob.

  • Non-personalised mode only. Google serves contextual ads based on coarse signals (your country, the app you're in) rather than a profile of you.
  • No App Tracking Transparency prompt. Your IDFA is not used by Vesper for tracking.
  • Google may still collect data necessary to serve and measure the ad: IP address, device type, app version, and a per-device anonymous identifier (IDFV). What Google does with that is governed by their own policy:
  • Google's Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use their services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Third-party services

  • Vesper astrology service — computes your chart and reading from the birth details you enter.
  • Google AdMob — banner advertising, non-personalised mode.

That's it. Vesper includes no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, and no other third-party tracking.

Children

Vesper is rated 4+ and does not knowingly target or collect data from children under 13.

Permissions

Vesper does not request any of the following: your current location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, App Tracking Transparency, notifications, or background activity.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published at the same URL where you are currently reading it, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
