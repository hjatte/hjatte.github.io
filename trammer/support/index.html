Do I need an account, or an internet connection?

Neither. Trammer has no accounts and no sign-in, and every city is baked into the app — so it works on a plane, on the Underground, and anywhere else with no signal.

How do I lay track?

Tap one point, then another. The track follows the street network between them, the way a real alignment has to, and the running total shows what it will cost before you commit. Done finishes the run; Undo takes back the last stretch and refunds it.

Can I lay track where there is no street?

Yes. Start on a street, then tap the street on the far side of a square, a park or the inside of a block, and the track cuts straight across the open ground between them. It costs more per kilometre than laying along a street, because new alignment does, and the game only offers it when going round would be meaningfully longer.

Why won't it lay track across that particular gap?

Something solid is in the way. A cut is refused if the straight line between the two points crosses a building or water, and both ends have to be a street — you cannot start a line in the middle of open ground. Very long gaps are refused too: beyond about 400 m it stops being a tram alignment.

Why is the tram taking such a long way round?

Because that is the shortest way the streets allow. Canal cities are the extreme case: a crossing that looks obvious on the map may have no bridge a tram could use. Cutting across open ground, above, is often the answer — and if the detour is truly absurd the game now says so and tells you the price before you buy it.

Which cities are free, and what is the City Pack?

Prague, Amsterdam and Toronto are free — chosen to do three different jobs rather than to be the three best boards. The City Pack is a single purchase that opens the other fourteen and lifts the budget limit.

I bought the City Pack and it hasn't appeared.

Open the city list and tap Restore purchases. That asks the App Store to re-check what your Apple Account owns. If it still doesn't appear, make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on.

What does a reserved lane do?

It takes your track out of general traffic. Track in a traffic lane is held up by the cars on it, so a reserved lane buys back journey time on the stretches where congestion is actually costing you — which is why the game will tell you which stretch is losing the most.

Why did my line's ridership fall after I extended it?

A longer line is not automatically a better one. Ridership follows where people actually want to go, and a detour that adds minutes for everyone already on board can cost more than the new stops bring in. The shape of the line is the decision, not its length.

How do I start a city again?

Open the city from the list and choose to reset it. That clears the network on that board only; the others keep theirs.

How do I delete my saved networks?

Delete the app. Your saves live only on your device, so they go with it — there is no copy in iCloud or anywhere else, and that also means they cannot be recovered afterwards.

The city map is wrong, or a street is missing.

The maps are built from OpenStreetMap, so a missing street is usually missing there too. Tell me which city and roughly where, and I will look at it on the next bake.
