TRAMMER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Trammer is a game about building a tram network on a city that is already there. This document describes what data the app does — and does not — collect.

Short version

  • Trammer collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no tracking across other apps.
  • It has no network code at all. The app contains no code that opens a network connection, so there is nothing for it to send and nowhere for it to send it. Every city is inside the app.
  • Your networks and settings are stored on your device only. They are not copied to iCloud or anywhere else, and I have no access to them.
  • The one purchase is handled by Apple's App Store. Trammer never sees your payment details.

What is stored

The following are stored on your device using the operating system's standard app storage:

  • Your saved networks — the track you have laid, your stops, your lines and your budget, for each city you have played
  • Which cities you have opened, and whether you own the City Pack
  • Your settings, such as sound, haptics and reduced motion

If you delete the app, all of it is removed with it. There is no copy anywhere else, so deleting the app deletes your networks permanently — I cannot recover them for you.

What is sent over the network

Nothing. Trammer has no server, no account system and no analytics, and the app contains no networking code of any kind. The city maps are baked into the app when it is built, so the game is complete the moment it is installed and works identically with the device in Airplane Mode.

The single exception is the App Store purchase described below, which is carried out by iOS rather than by this app.

Where the maps come from

The streets, blocks, buildings and water in each city are built from OpenStreetMap data, processed into the game's own format before the app is released. This happens on my machine, not on your device: the app never contacts OpenStreetMap, and nothing about where you are or what you look at is sent to them or to anyone else. OpenStreetMap data is © OpenStreetMap contributors and used under the Open Database Licence.

Purchases

Trammer offers one purchase, the City Pack, which opens the cities beyond the three free ones. The transaction is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app receives only a confirmation from the operating system that the purchase belongs to the Apple Account signed in on the device; it never sees your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

Third-party services

None. Trammer includes no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting service, no advertising SDK and no other third-party tracking.

Children

Trammer is rated 4+ and does not knowingly target or collect data from children under 13. Since the app collects no data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Permissions

None. Trammer does not request notifications, your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, App Tracking Transparency, or background activity.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in the future, the updated version will be published at the same URL where you are currently reading it, and the “Last updated” date above will change.
